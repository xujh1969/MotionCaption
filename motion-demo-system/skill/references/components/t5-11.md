# t5-11 - 气泡集群模块(大小差异化圆形气泡·数组扩展)

- Component version: 1
- Use for: Multi-entity ecosystems such as business sectors, project clusters, partner networks, or industrial matrices shown as sized bubbles at the screen side.
- Avoid: Avoid strictly ordered timelines, more than 6 bubbles, and long descriptions beyond three lines inside a bubble.
- Motion feel: 气泡按数组顺序依次出现，每个 at 独立控制：圆形由中心向外缩放浮现并淡入，随后标题淡入、描述跟进；激活气泡呼吸缩放（1.0~1.04）且外发光 0.25~0.4 循环，已完成的弱光效静止；结尾 700ms 逆序缩小淡出。
- Capacity: 2-6 items

## Editable content

Only the following keys may appear in `content`. All style and component layout properties are locked and must not be supplied by an Agent.

- `items`: array of objects { desc: 描述 (string), title: 标题 (string) }; required: yes; default: `[{"desc":"智能制造产业园：总投资40亿，入驻企业150家","title":"星动智谷"},{"desc":"产学研平台，联合5所高校","title":"皓域创研院"},{"desc":"文化科技专项基金—已投12个文创项目","title":"Innov8 基金"}]`; semantic role: items; constraints: 2-6 items; all listed item fields are required strings.
